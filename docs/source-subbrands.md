# El Chico Sub-Brands Audit (Phase 0.5)

The source website relies heavily on grouping content into three sub-brands rather than generic sections. This document verifies their existence and features.

## 1. El Chico Restaurant
- **Role:** The core fine dining and flagship restaurant experience.
- **Route:** `/elchico-restaurant/`
- **Menu:** Relies on `elchico-a_compressed.pdf` and `Elchico-Restaurant-.pdf`
- **Imagery:** Features the original logo and classic interior shots (e.g., `elrestro.jpg`, `rooftoprest.jpg`).
- **Target Audience:** Premium dining, heritage-focused (Since 1964).

## 2. The Baking Co (TBC)
- **Role:** The bakery, patisserie, and cafe segment.
- **Route:** `/the-baking-co/`
- **Menu:** Relies on `TBC-Menu-1.pdf` and `TBC-Civil-Lines-Menu-.pdf`
- **Imagery:** Has a distinct black-and-white inline SVG logo (`tbclogoblack`) and baked goods imagery (`bakebanner1.jpg`).
- **Vibe:** Modern, casual, sweet-focused.

## 3. Veggie Veg (VV)
- **Role:** The dedicated vegetarian offering.
- **Route:** `/veggie-veg/`
- **Menu:** Relies on `VV-Elchico.pdf` and an educational PDF `Vegetarianism-4-converted.pdf`.
- **Imagery:** Features specific vegetarian marketing materials (`veggiepage.jpg`, `veggiepage2.jpg`).
- **Focus:** Pure vegetarian dining, likely catering to specific cultural/dietary preferences in the region.

---

**Redesign Takeaway:**
We must NOT collapse these into a single generic "Menu" section. The architecture of the new site must clearly differentiate these three pillars, ideally through art direction, routing, or interactive tabs/cards that respect their distinct identities while unifying them under the El Chico master brand.
